# D. E. Shaw Group engineering track

Sits beside [quant.md](quant.md) and [hrt.md](hrt.md): **computational-finance SWE / systems (NYC / Hyderabad / …)**, not Jane Street’s official “no LeetCode / no mental math” pair loop and not a market-maker OA-then-trading-system day. Official [interviewing guide](https://www.deshaw.com/careers/interviewing-guide) + [interviewing](https://www.deshaw.com/careers/interviewing): **no prior finance knowledge required**. Recruiter confirms **Software Developer vs Quant Systems vs Infrastructure / AppEng vs research**, language, office, and AI (ask — they do not publish a live-AI rule on the guide).

Typical official path: **application review → phone → virtual interviews → references → offer**. They may also ask for a **code sample, writing sample, or case study**. Official: several weeks; they will not shortcut recruiting. Pair flavor: [../general/pair-programming.md](../general/pair-programming.md). CS: [../general/cs-fundamentals.md](../general/cs-fundamentals.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (five core principles)

Do **not** invent Amazon-style LPs. Official [Who we are / core principles](https://www.deshaw.com/who-we-are/core-principles). Map **your** stories.

| Official principle | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Hire and cultivate extraordinary people** | Raw ability + long-term potential, not a finance pedigree |
| **Take the high road** | Ethics, respect, letter **and** spirit of the law |
| **Set unusually ambitious goals** | Tries others called infeasible; one breakthrough can pay for many misses |
| **Focus on the firm as a whole** | Collective contribution over local empire |
| **Analyze rigorously and communicate clearly** | Systematic planning; precise speech |

Official interviewing page’s “what we’re looking for” maps onto the same ideas: high standards, rigorous analysis, concise communication, collaboration, intellectual curiosity. “Why DESCO?” that only says “quant shop, high TC” fails — name a **systems, modeling, or developer-tooling** problem you have lived.

Official Software Developer JD: technology is **integral to virtually everything** the firm does — statistical models, real-time distributed ingest, math-modeling tools. Developer Experience JD (2026): IDEs, build, CI/CD, **evaluate and champion AI-powered developer tools**. Quant Systems: HPC, kernels, clustered FS, low-latency tune. Ask which **org**.

## Official process

Official [interviewing guide](https://www.deshaw.com/careers/interviewing-guide). They score **how you process new information**, not a single “correct” trick or a specific language.

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Application review | Human process; several weeks is normal |
| Phone + virtual interviews | Behavioral, situational, and **case studies** (discussion, write-up, or role-like exercise) |
| Technical (SWE / quant / software) | Math and/or CS **plus** behavioral: algorithms, probability/stats, logical thinking, programming — **techniques, uses, and limits** |
| Systems-focused | CS/programming **or** subject expertise: complex processes, **distributed-systems interactions**, operational problem-solving |
| References, then offer | Official last steps; recruiter owns the clock |

Official case-study method they want spoken: understand the question → structure → **state assumptions** → enumerate approaches and tradeoffs → support with analysis → **next steps and risks**. Same “show your work” for behavioral. Official: you do **not** need a particular programming language; they care that you know the tools you pick.

## Coding and design flavor

Official SWE: models, real-time distributed analysis, modeling tools. Systems: schedulers, fleet, storage, kernel/network tune. Live: algorithms + language fluency + **limits of the technique**. Design: what you lock on a feed, what happens when a cluster or venue dies. Related: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md), [../answers/system-design-metrics.md](../answers/system-design-metrics.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** DESCO org — a modeling, HPC, or developer-experience call.
2. Case: weigh options, name data you would collect, state the assumption that would flip the call.
3. CS: an algorithm you know **and** when it is the wrong tool.
4. Principles: high road under pressure, or a miss that was ambitious on purpose.
5. Questions: SWE vs systems vs DevEx, AI tooling, which city, sample vs live code.
