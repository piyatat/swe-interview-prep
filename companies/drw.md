# DRW engineering track

Sits beside [hrt.md](hrt.md) and [optiver.md](optiver.md): **prop-trading SWE (Chicago HQ + US / Canada / Europe / APAC)**, not HRT’s official HRTBeat take-home-plus-systems day and not a published “no LeetCode” pair loop. Official [Work at DRW](https://www.drw.com/work-at-drw): **low ego, little hierarchy, continuous learning**. Recruiter confirms **Software Developer vs quant / researcher / trader**, language, office, and AI (ask — they do not publish a live-AI rule on the careers pages).

Typical official path (graduates + new-grad developer write-ups): **rolling resume → timed take-home challenge → ~45-min Zoom → two-day onsite**. Official: they are **agile** — your loop may differ; recruiter wins. Pair flavor: [../general/pair-programming.md](../general/pair-programming.md). CS: [../general/cs-fundamentals.md](../general/cs-fundamentals.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture

Do **not** invent Amazon-style LPs. Official [Work at DRW](https://www.drw.com/work-at-drw):

| Official signal | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Integrity** | Transparency; hold yourself and others accountable |
| **Critical thought** | Technical / research debate; question what you think you know |
| **Learning** | Formal + day-to-day growth |
| **Challenge consensus** | Strong opinions, loosely held |
| **Preparation** | Expected **and** unexpected; research as markets move |
| **Respect** | Diverse room; collaborate thoughtfully |
| **Sense of urgency** | Priorities shift; adjust quickly |

Official SWE JD: hire exceptional talent, empower bold thinking, reward impact. Developers sit with **technologists, researchers, and traders** across the stack; **meaningful responsibility from day one** plus mentorship and a training program. New-grad blog: they want **confidence, humility, curiosity, and a teammate**. “Why DRW?” that only says “Chicago / prop / TC” fails — name a **latency, data, or distributed** problem you have lived.

## Official process

Official [graduates](https://www.drw.com/work-at-drw/graduates) + [new-grad developer](https://www.drw.com/updates/insights/how-to-become-a-successful-new-grad-developer-at-drw) + [phone-interview tips](https://www.drw.com/updates/insights/acing-your-phone-interview). Experienced loops are not separately published — **ask**.

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Application | Rolling resume review; apply early while the req is up |
| Technical challenge | At home, **timed**. SWE: CS fundamentals coding. Quant / research: probability + logic. Do **not** mix tracks |
| Zoom (~45 min) | A **developer, trader, or researcher** (not a sourcer). Technical **and** behavioral. SWE: have a **computer ready** for coding. Resume deep-dive — they will ask about **everything** on it |
| Final onsite | **Day 1 afternoon:** intros + another technical challenge. **Day 2:** full day technical + behavioral; network with future teammates |
| Decision | After the onsite |

Official phone tips: reply with **three weekday 9–5** slots (no weekends); quiet room; charger; prepare **questions** — you are talking to the people you would work with.

## How this track differs

| vs HRT / Optiver / Flow Traders | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **timed take-home + two-day onsite** | Seven named signals; low-ego is explicit |
| Phone is a **peer** (dev / trader / researcher) | Markets-adjacent systems, not “design Twitter” |
| New-grad blog names a second **onsite coding challenge** | “I only grind Blind 75 silently” misses resume depth + culture |

## Coding and design flavor

Official JD: full-stack problems that need analysis **and** swift execution — low-latency trading, data, distributed systems, performance-critical infra (from the careers framing). Live: fundamental CS, then how the code sits next to a trader. Design: shared book or feed; what you lock; what happens when a venue or cluster dies. Related: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md), [../answers/system-design-metrics.md](../answers/system-design-metrics.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** DRW team — a systems or data call, not “trading is cool.”
2. Timed take-home: CS fundamentals; treat it as a filter, not a take-home novel.
3. Zoom: resume line-by-line + a live coding question on your machine.
4. Onsite: another coding challenge plus culture (challenge consensus, urgency).
5. Questions: SWE vs quant track, which city, AI, whether experienced skips the take-home.
