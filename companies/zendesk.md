# Zendesk engineering track

Sits beside [hubspot.md](hubspot.md), [salesforce.md](salesforce.md), and [servicenow.md](servicenow.md): **AI-for-service / ticketing + omnichannel**, not inbound CRM and not ITSM workflows-as-code. Official [Careers](https://www.zendesk.com/company/careers/): mission is **exceptional service for everyone on the planet**; they pitch the “most successful AI-for-service company.” Recruiter confirms **CX platform vs Copilot / AI agents vs EX**, hub (SF, Kraków, Pune, Lisbon, …), and take-home vs live.

Typical timeline **~3–5 weeks** (2026 guides). OA / take-home: [../general/take-homes.md](../general/take-homes.md). Multi-tenant SaaS: [../general/system-design.md](../general/system-design.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official hire bar (do not invent extra pillars)

Official [Careers — What we stand for](https://www.zendesk.com/company/careers/) lists **four** principles. Map **your** stories.

| Official principle | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead with exceptional service** | Customer + teammate service, not ticket-count vanity |
| **Deliver the simplest solution** | Smallest safe path; you cut scope |
| **Take pride in our work** | Craft you would put your name on |
| **Care for each other** | Empathy, team player; they say culture is **who you work with** |

Careers also: hybrid “in person where it helps”; **everyone uses AI on real work** (training provided) — that is **not** a live-pad permit. Confirm AI-in-eval.

“Why Zendesk?” that only says “I like support tools / AI” fails. Name a **ticket, SLA, or omnichannel** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Official [How we hire](https://www.zendesk.com/company/careers/): process **varies by role**; generally four stages.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter interview | Background, motivations, the role |
| Hiring manager interview | Experience, achievements, skills for the role |
| Technical or skills assessment | Official: case study, **coding challenge**, or presentation. Guides: practical take-home / OA (API / parse), not puzzle DSA |
| Final panel | Team + culture fit. Guides: pair, system design, behavioral |

2026 Dataford / PracHub: treat the **pair + design + behavioral** onsite as **reported**. Official Zendesk **Customer Operations** Karat pages describe a live programming hour (functional solutions first, Big-O second) — **ask if your SWE loop uses Karat**; do not assume the CX-ops script.

Confirm languages, AI-in-pad, and whether the assessment is take-home vs live.

## How this track differs

| vs HubSpot / Salesforce / ServiceNow | vs FAANG web |
| --- | --- |
| Official **four principles**; domain is **service / tickets** | Design is **omnichannel + SLA + AI agent**, not a feed |
| Practical assessment more than a CodeSignal GCA (guides) | “I only grind LeetCode” misses simplest-solution stories |
| Global hubs; hybrid by team | Ask Karat vs take-home; do not assume an AI hour |

## Coding and design flavor

Official products: ticketing, messaging, contact center, AI agents, Copilot, knowledge. Live work: REST + webhooks, idempotent updates, routing, multi-tenant isolation. Related: [../answers/system-design-notification.md](../answers/system-design-notification.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** service platform — portable “SaaS / AI” is weak.
2. Practical: parse a ticket stream or webhook; keep updates idempotent.
3. Design: route tickets + AI-agent draft + human handoff; SLA clocks.
4. Principle: simplest solution — you deleted a feature instead of adding one.
5. Questions for them: take-home vs Karat, panel shape, hub days, AI-in-eval.

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Read [Careers / How we hire](https://www.zendesk.com/company/careers/) (mission + four principles)
