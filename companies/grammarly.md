# Superhuman (Grammarly) engineering track

Sits beside [ai-labs.md](ai-labs.md), [notion.md](notion.md), and [monday.md](monday.md): **AI writing + agents across the work graph**, not a research lab and not a Work OS. Official Nov 2025 rebrand: the **company** is [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/company/careers) (suite: Grammarly, Docs/Coda, Mail, Go). Recruiter confirms **product vs infra vs agents**, hub vs remote, and whether they still say “Grammarly.”

Typical timeline **~3–6 weeks** (guides; official FAQ does not pin a clock). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Design: [../general/system-design.md](../general/system-design.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official hire bar (do not invent extra pillars)

Official [Mission & Values](https://superhuman.com/company/values). Map **your** stories. The older [interviewing guide](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/interviewing/interviewing-at-grammarly/) still scores **EAGER** (Ethical, Adaptable, Gritty, Empathetic, Remarkable) and [MOVE](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/company/move-operating-principles/) — **ask** which rubric your loop uses.

| Official Superhuman value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **(Super) Human-Centered** | You designed for judgment / workflow, not “replace the writer” |
| **Win with Grit** | You stayed through the messy middle and shipped |
| **Craft Remarkable Delight** | The UX felt effortless; you killed a clever-but-frictiony path |
| **Think from First Principles** | You dropped a template and rebuilt from constraints |
| **Grow Ideas from Anywhere** | A quieter voice changed the design; you made space for it |
| **Prioritize the Pond** | Collective good over local team scoreboard |

“Why Superhuman / Grammarly?” that only says “I like writing AI / I used the extension” fails. Name a **suggestion latency, privacy, agent-handoff, or context-stitching** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Official [interviewing guide](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/interviewing/interviewing-at-grammarly/) (still live under the Grammarly blog) is the skeleton. 2025 candidate reports add seat splits — treat extras as **reported**.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Official: role, life at the company, experience; they send **interview guides + prep sessions** |
| Hiring manager | Official: past work, impact, work style, team vision |
| Technical | Official: programming, algorithms / DS, **system design**; recruiter names the pad |
| Behavioral | Official: STAR vs **EAGER** (and possibly MOVE) |
| Loop shape (reported) | Two coding hours, design, project-dive / impact, dedicated behavioral; possible extra HM / exec |

Confirm AI-in-pad. Official Superhuman Careers: nine hubs (SF, NYC, Seattle, Mountain View, Vancouver, Berlin, Kyiv, Porto, Warsaw); **opt-in** office days with scaled wellness stipends — not a silent five-day RTO.

## How this track differs

| vs OpenAI / Anthropic | vs Notion / Coda-only |
| --- | --- |
| Official **human-centered AI** bar; privacy / “we do not sell your writing” is on-message | Product is **write + inbox + docs + agents**, not a block canvas alone |
| Grit / delight / pond — not a research-paper hour | “I only grind LeetCode” misses the project-dive |
| Distributed hubs + opt-in office | Silent “replace the writer” fails Human-Centered |

## Coding and design flavor

Official product: Grammarly writing, Coda/Docs, Mail, Superhuman Go + Agent Store / SDK. Live work: suggestion latency, PII, evals on tone / factuality, connector agents, fan-out. Related: [../answers/system-design-notification.md](../answers/system-design-notification.md), [../answers/system-design-llm-serving.md](../answers/system-design-llm-serving.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** suite — portable “AI writing is hot” is weak.
2. Value: Prioritize the Pond — you killed a local win that hurt the platform.
3. Coding: merge suggestions from two sources without duplicating spans.
4. Design: a writing agent that must not leak tenant context across tabs.
5. Questions for them: EAGER vs Superhuman values, AI-in-pad, hub days, Coda vs Grammarly team.
