# Morgan Stanley engineering track

Sits beside [goldman.md](goldman.md) and [jpmorgan.md](jpmorgan.md): **integrated-firm technology (markets, wealth, platforms)**, not Goldman’s tenets Superday and not JPMC’s cohort Super Day + match. Official [core values](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us/morgan-stanley-core-values): **first-class business in a first-class way** since 1935. Recruiter confirms **campus vs experienced**, **Application Development vs Enterprise Engineering vs analytics**, location, and whether the first gate is an **OA / HireVue**.

Typical reported path **~4–8+ weeks** (2026 guides; interviewing.io: often **>6 weeks**, decentralized by team). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). LLD: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md). SQL: [../general/sql-interviews.md](../general/sql-interviews.md). ## Official culture (five core values)

Do **not** invent extra pillars. Official [Core Values](https://www.morganstanley.com/about-us/morgan-stanley-core-values). Map **your** stories.

| Official value | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Do the Right Thing** | Integrity; think like an owner; honesty and character |
| **Put Clients First** | Client interest; deliver the best of the Firm; listen |
| **Lead with Exceptional Ideas** | Break new ground; other perspectives; stay vigilant |
| **Commit to Diversity & Inclusion** | Differences as strength; belonging; challenge exclusion |
| **Give Back** | Communities; culture; mentor and sponsor talent |

Official [Interview Preparation](https://www.morganstanley.com/people-opportunities/students-graduates/resources/interview-preparation): they want **who you are and what motivates you**, plus whether you understand the **job and the Firm**. Know your résumé cold. Interviews may be competency, strengths, skills, or technical. Have a reason for **this industry, this Firm, and this division** — generic “What does Morgan Stanley do?” questions fail. They do **not** expect you to know every market; pick one or two areas you can discuss with conviction.

“Why Morgan Stanley?” that only says “bulge bracket / finance pays” fails. Name a **client-correctness, risk, or platform** problem you have lived.

## Official process (campus is published)

Official [Australia recruitment](https://www.morganstanley.com.au/careers/recruitment-process) + [students FAQ](https://www.morganstanley.com/people-opportunities/students-graduates/resources/questions) + China campus FAQ (2026 cycle):

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | Careers site (some NA roles also on the school board — apply **both**) |
| Pre-interview test | **Some divisions** test before first-round interviews |
| First round | Phone, video, or campus: **technical + competency + motivational** (some regions add language) |
| Finals | In-person or virtual: 1:1s, technicals, and **group exercises** depending on division |

China campus FAQ: online **technical test** → phone → final online (or Shanghai office). Experienced-hire **round list is not official**. 2026 guides (TechPrep, interviewing.io) — treat as **reported**: recruiter, HackerRank / Aon + HireVue on some teams, 1–2 live technicals (often **Java internals**), then a Superday / onsite (coding, OOP hour, optional design, values). Team-owned; you can interview **more than one** team.

## How this track differs

| vs Goldman / JPMC | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **five values**; process is **team-decentralized** | Design is **ledger / cache / inbox**, not a social feed |
| Campus: possible **group exercise** + OA | Guides: Java / OOP / SQL sit beside DSA |
| “Why this division?” is on the official prep page | “I only grind hards” misses the values + ethics hour |

## Coding and design flavor

Guides: medium arrays / hashes / windows / trees; JVM `volatile` / locks; SQL joins and indexes; LLD with concurrency (cache, money movement). Related: [../answers/system-design-payment.md](../answers/system-design-payment.md), [../answers/coding-lru-cache.md](../answers/coding-lru-cache.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** division (markets vs wealth vs enterprise) — portable “I like fintech” is weak.
2. Official: walk a résumé bullet without looking; what you would develop next.
3. Coding: medium window / tree; then “why this structure?”
4. OOP: abstract class vs interface; when you would lock vs `volatile`.
5. Do the Right Thing: a time you refused to skip a control for a date.
