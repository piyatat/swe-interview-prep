# Roku engineering track

Sits beside [netflix.md](netflix.md) and [disney.md](disney.md): **streaming OS / player / ads platform** (Roku OS, channel store, OneView ads) shipped to **devices in living rooms**, not a studio catalog loop and not a CDN-only hour. Official [Jobs](https://www.weareroku.com/): power every TV; hybrid Mon–Thu in office, flexible Friday. Recruiter confirms **player / OS / cloud / ads / data**, hub (San Jose, Cambridge, Bengaluru, …), language (often C/C++ on device), and **AI-in-pad**.

Typical timeline **~3–4 weeks** (official Bengaluru FAQ). Streaming design: [../answers/system-design-video-streaming.md](../answers/system-design-video-streaming.md). Device / firmware: [../roles/embedded.md](../roles/embedded.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture

Official [Roku Culture](https://www.weareroku.com/roku-culture). Map **your** stories. Do not recite. Hiring bar on the same page: **how good are they at the job** and **do they fit the culture**.

| Official theme | Culture page one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **High performance** | Recruit carefully, pay market, expect a high bar; independent thinkers who still ship as a team |
| **Trust & responsibility** | Decide with enough consensus, then **commit**; untracked vacation if the work is done |
| **Talent / no-ego** | Fewer very strong people; easy to work with; humor; **no-ego zone** (Bengaluru FAQ) |
| **Communication & context** | Plans and bad news are visible; email-heavy; be present in meetings or skip them |
| **World-class product** | Ease of use, value, content; protect IP with partners |

“Why Roku?” that only says “I like streaming / smart TVs” fails. Name a **device-constrained player, channel catalog, ad-pod, or 100M-household rollout** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Official FAQ: recruiter (30 min) → first interview → loop → feedback call → offer. Careers does **not** name every technical hour. 2026 guides (TechPrep) + Glassdoor — treat extra seats as **reported**. Confirm.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Background, team, hub, comp; ~3–4 weeks end-to-end |
| First interview | Often live **HackerRank** coding (reports: medium arrays / maps / DP / graphs) |
| Loop | Coding + HLD (player / CDN / ads) + **LLD** + culture with a senior |
| AI (official, Jul 2026) | Prep with AI is fine; **no AI in live interviews** unless the JD says so |

Official [Using AI in Talent Acquisition](https://www.weareroku.com/using-ai-in-roku-talent-acquisition) (updated 2026-07-02): research and clarity yes; inventing experience or live-interview copilots no. Some early-career **AI/UI** JDs flip that: they **score** a hands-on hour on an unfamiliar repo **with** your usual AI tools. Ask which seat you have.

## How this track differs

| vs Netflix / Disney+ | vs generic FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Client is a **TV OS + ads platform**, not only origin + CDN | Official culture page is the behavioral spec; no Amazon LPs |
| Device RAM/CPU and OTA blast radius show up in design | HackerRank live coding; some interviewers go quiet — narrate anyway |
| Hybrid four days; Bengaluru hub is published | A few roles **insist** on AI-in-the-loop; most live hours are AI-off |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** streaming platform — device + ads constraint, not “I watch Roku.”
2. Design: start playback of a channel with ads; CDN miss; bitrate ladder on a low-RAM stick.
3. LLD: channel-subscription or ad-pod scheduler; class boundaries, not a slogan.
4. Trust: you decided with incomplete consensus, then the team executed.
5. Questions for them: HackerRank vs skip, C++ vs backend, office days, AI-in-pad for **this** JD.
