# Epic Systems engineering track

Sits beside [product-companies.md](product-companies.md) and [ibm.md](ibm.md): **enterprise EHR / clinical software** (Verona, WI campus), not a consumer-app or consulting loop. Official [Application Process](https://careers.epic.com/applicationprocess/) is the hire skeleton. Official [Careers](https://careers.epic.com/): **325 million** patients and their doctors depend on software that works. Recruiter / HR confirm **developer vs other**, **OA shape**, Verona vs virtual final, and **whether outside aid is allowed** on each artifact.

Typical timeline **4–8 weeks** (2026 guides). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). OA: [../general/online-assessments.md](../general/online-assessments.md). ## Official culture (mission + ownership)

Do **not** invent Amazon-style LPs. Use official careers language, then map **your** stories.

| Official note | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Patient / clinician impact** (careers) | Software that lets clinicians deliver care — not “I like healthcare startups” |
| **Curiosity, ingenuity, leadership, perseverance** (careers) | Ownership of an application area; freedom to decide |
| **Mentorship + ongoing learning** (careers) | Campus classes, UW–Madison faculty CS, pay-it-forward |
| **Honesty at every stage** (application process) | Resume, OA, interviews, offer, pre-onboarding — ask HR if aid is unclear |

Official [Principles of Our Community](https://careers.epic.com/dei/): dignity and respect; welcome contributions; bias in **and around** the software; growth support; value other perspectives. “Why Epic?” that only says “stable job / Madison” fails. Name a **correctness, workflow, or interoperability** problem you have lived — Epic hires **generalists**; prior EHR experience is not the bar.

## Official + reported process

Official process has **three** published beats. Role variants exist. 2025–2026 candidate reports add a personality inventory, a multi-part OA, and a long virtual / Verona final — treat extra hours as **reported**.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Quick application | Resume + a few brief questions (official) |
| Skills assessments | **Logic and reasoning**; official: **no prep work** |
| Final interview | Get to know you + the job (official) |
| Reported OA extras | Timed logic; untimed puzzles; easy–medium coding (sometimes **no run**); invented-language reasoning; “Rembrandt” style inventory |
| Reported final | Company / role overview, 45-min design or case, 45-min coding or pair, HM / HR; some fly to **Verona** |

Official: if you are unsure whether **outside aid** is permitted on an assessment, ask your HR contact. Guides (techinterview.org): aptitude / logic is a **high-leverage gate**; coding is easy–medium; culture and relocation-to-campus matter as much as stack. Confirm live vs recorded, pair vs solo, and AI policy the week you sit.

## How this track differs

| vs FAANG / product SaaS | vs IBM / hospital IT |
| --- | --- |
| Official OA is **logic + reasoning**, not a CodeSignal GCA clone | You ship **one clinical application** for years, not a two-sprint feature |
| Campus / Verona identity; relocation is common | Design is **orders, charting, identity, downtime**, not “design Twitter” |
| Generalist hire; MUMPS / Caché / IRIS appear **on the job** more than as a pre-req | Honesty policy is **written** — do not silently use AI on the OA |

## Coding and design flavor

Reported coding: arrays, strings, graphs at easy–medium; some finals are pair-programming. Design / case: clinical workflow, downtime, identity matching, audit. Do not bluff HIPAA trivia you cannot defend. Related: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md), [../general/sql-interviews.md](../general/sql-interviews.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** EHR — a correctness call that protected a user (or a patient-adjacent system).
2. OA: timed logic without a coding editor; then a short implement with no “run all tests” crutch.
3. Design: an order that must not double-fire after a timeout; who sees it; what is audited.
4. Campus: a time you owned a messy workflow end-to-end, not a ticket slice.
5. Questions for them: OA sections this year, Verona vs virtual, AI / outside-aid rules, application area.
