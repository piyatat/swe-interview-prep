# eBay engineering track

Sits beside [product-companies.md](product-companies.md) and [instacart.md](instacart.md): **two-sided marketplace** (listings, search, trust, payments), not a first-party inventory slate. Official [How we hire](https://jobs.ebayinc.com/us/en/how-we-hire): **people-first**; AI organizes applications, humans decide. Most interviews **virtual on Zoom**; engineering may include a **coding assessment**. Official: AI to **prepare**, not to generate answers in interviews or assessments; **camera on**. BrightHire may record/transcribe — you can **opt out** with no penalty; it does **not** score or rank. Recruiter confirms **OA vs live**, search vs checkout vs trust loop, and recording consent.

Typical timeline **4–8 weeks** (2026 guides). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Search: [../answers/system-design-search.md](../answers/system-design-search.md). Payments: [../answers/system-design-payment.md](../answers/system-design-payment.md). Ranking: [../answers/system-design-recommendation.md](../answers/system-design-recommendation.md).

## Official culture (purpose + DNA)

Do **not** recite posters. Map **your** stories. Official [Our Culture](https://jobs.ebayinc.com/us/en/our-culture) + [Culture & Workforce](https://www.ebayinc.com/impact/culture-and-workforce/): purpose is **connect people and build communities to create economic opportunity for all**. Five beliefs (“Our DNA”) — names as in eBay’s 10-K / careers restatements:

| Official DNA | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Empower our Community** | Customer at the heart; trusted experiences; sellers as partners |
| **Innovate Boldly** | Think big; curiosity; learn from succeed **or** fail |
| **Be for Everyone** | Inclusive teams + marketplace for buyers and sellers everywhere |
| **Deliver with Impact** | Debate, then commit; urgency; own results |
| **Act with Integrity** | People are basically good; honest especially when hard |

“Why eBay?” that only says “I shop there” fails. Name a **trust, search, or two-sided** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Official How-we-hire is the skeleton. 2026 guides (Dataford, CleverPrep) stretch it — treat round counts as **reported**.

| Stage | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| Apply (official) | Humans decide; AI highlights JD-relevant details; some roles start with a questionnaire |
| Recruiter + HM + teammates (official) | Two-way; mission fit; collaboration |
| Technical (official: possible) | Coding assessment and/or live screen, case, or skills exercise |
| Loop (guides) | More coding / debug, marketplace design, DNA behavioral |
| Evaluate (official) | Structured interviewer feedback; optional panel; humans hire |
| Offer (official) | **Verbal first**, then letter; background checks by role/region |

Official three guidelines: honest materials; no AI during scored interviews/assessments; camera on for virtual.

## How this track differs

| vs FAANG | vs Instacart / DoorDash |
| --- | --- |
| Official **BrightHire opt-out** + AI-prepare-only | Grocery / dispatch marketplaces; eBay is **listings + unique inventory** |
| Design is **search, trust, two-sided payments**, not “design Twitter” | Same marketplace bar; eBay adds **authenticity / safety** |
| People review packets; AI does not rank (official) | Pair [../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md](../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md) |

## Coding and design flavor

DSA: medium arrays / maps / graphs; guides also mention debug. Design: listing search + ranking, seller trust / fraud queues, bid or Best Offer, inventory that is **not** fungible SKUs. Related: [../answers/system-design-ticketmaster.md](../answers/system-design-ticketmaster.md) (named uniqueness), [../answers/system-design-notification.md](../answers/system-design-notification.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. “Act with Integrity — you stopped a ship that would have burned seller trust.”
2. Live: transform listing / bid events; narrate edge cases (withdrawn bid, ended listing).
3. Design search over unique inventory: relevance vs freshness vs trust signals.
4. How do you keep buyer and seller ledgers consistent after a payment dispute?
5. Official AI page: how you used AI to **practice**, and what you would not paste into a model.
