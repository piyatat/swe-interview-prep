# Faire engineering track

Sits beside [etsy.md](etsy.md), [shopify.md](shopify.md), and [affirm.md](affirm.md): **two-sided wholesale marketplace + net-term credit**, not DTC e-commerce and not a consumer BNPL loop. Official [Careers](https://www.faire.com/careers): small businesses are the reason they come to work; they “build the technology that powers local retail.” Recruiter confirms **search / growth vs payments / credit vs marketplace vs ML**, Kotlin vs TypeScript, and whether you get CodeSignal.

Typical timeline **3–4 weeks** (2026 guides). OA: [../general/online-assessments.md](../general/online-assessments.md). Marketplace cousins: [../answers/system-design-recommendation.md](../answers/system-design-recommendation.md), [../answers/system-design-payment.md](../answers/system-design-payment.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official hire bar (do not invent extra pillars)

Official Careers lists **five** values. Map **your** stories.

| Official value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **We serve our community** | Customer-obsessed; retailer **and** brand success |
| **We make it happen (fast)** | High urgency; resourceful; fail, then find a way |
| **We raise the bar** | Humble, hungry; craft and standards keep moving |
| **We seek the truth** | Trial/error **or** analysis — whichever is fastest; inconvenient facts win |
| **We are one Faire** | Low ego, candor, company + community over self |

“Why Faire?” that only says “wholesale Shopify / I like marketplaces” fails. Name a **two-sided liquidity, search freshness, or credit-limit** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Faire’s [Craft](https://craft.faire.com/joining-faire-as-a-software-engineer-1d7d56de0921) SWE join note (employee, official blog): language-**agnostic** screen (use the language you know; Kotlin/React is the house stack, not the pad requirement), then **two** 1-hour coding/algorithms interviews plus a **30-minute values** interview.

2026 TechInterview — treat extra stages as **reported** (confirm with recruiter):

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen (guides) | Background, level, remote US/CA vs hub (SF, NYC, Toronto, KW) |
| CodeSignal / coding phone (Craft + guides) | Medium DSA; guides also name a 60-min phone |
| Virtual onsite (guides) | More coding, marketplace design, craft, behavioral |
| Values alignment (Craft: 30 min) | Five values with **your** stories |

Guides: designs lean **variable-inventory search**, **net-60 credit limits**, and “retailers like you bought.” Coding often wears a marketplace costume (dedupe, graphs, union-find). Do not invent a published LP list beyond the five values.

## How this track differs

| vs Etsy / Shopify / Affirm | vs FAANG web |
| --- | --- |
| Official **five values**; Craft says **any language** on the pad | Design is **B2B wholesale + terms**, not “design Twitter” |
| Two-sided catalog + Faire-fronted payables (guides) | “I only grind LeetCode” misses community + truth-seeking |
| Handshake / DTC stories are cousins, not substitutes | Ask AI-in-pad; do not assume an AI hour |

## Coding and design flavor

DSA: graphs / UF (account merge), intervals, ranking windows. Design: cold-start brands, inventory that turns daily, credit limits that update on repayment, geo-relevant recs. Related: [../answers/coding-accounts-merge.md](../answers/coding-accounts-merge.md), [../answers/system-design-search.md](../answers/system-design-search.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** wholesale marketplace — portable “e-commerce” is weak.
2. Live: group merged retailer accounts (UF) or rank a freshness-heavy list.
3. Design: net-60 limit for a new retailer — signals, update loop, missed payment.
4. Value: Seek the truth — you killed a favorite metric when the data said so.
5. Questions for them: CodeSignal vs live, Kotlin vs TS, credit vs search team, AI policy.
