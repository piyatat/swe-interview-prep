# Target engineering track

Sits beside [walmart.md](walmart.md) and [chewy.md](chewy.md): **general-merchandise retail + Target.com / supply chain (Minneapolis HQ)**, not Walmart’s Global Tech Superday and not Chewy’s pet-autoship loop. Official purpose ([Code of Ethics](https://corporate.target.com/getmedia/498e5ee6-0d90-4a00-a462-97562b7d2723/Target-Code-of-Ethics.pdf)): **help all families discover the joy of everyday life**. Recruiter confirms **corporate / Target Tech vs store vs FC**, hub, stack, and whether the first hour is **HireVue**.

Typical reported path **~2–4 weeks** (2026 guides). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Debug / pair: [../general/debugging-rounds.md](../general/debugging-rounds.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (purpose + three values)

Do **not** invent Amazon LPs. Official [culture](https://corporate.target.com/careers/culture) is **Care, Grow, and Win Together**. Official values (Code of Ethics / 2024 sustainability report) are how they score stories. Official [interview guide](https://corporate.target.com/careers/hiring-process/interview-guide) maps sample questions to the same ideas.

| Official line | Careers / ethics one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Help all families discover the joy of everyday life |
| **Inclusivity** | Diverse voices; authentic; equitable experiences |
| **Connection** | Trusted relationships; collaborate across functions |
| **Drive** | Right for Target / team / guest; results; progress over perfection |
| **Care / Grow / Win** | Show up; develop people; win the right way as a team |

“Why Target?” that only says “retail / Minneapolis / I shop there” fails. Name a **guest, inventory, fulfillment, or store-tech** problem you have lived.

Official [hiring process](https://corporate.target.com/careers/hiring-process) **Use of AI**: live and recorded answers must be **your** knowledge — no external AI-generated assistance (approved accommodations excepted).

## Official process

Official hiring process: apply in **Workday** → recruiting review → one or more of **phone / virtual, in-person, recorded video**. Official [recorded video](https://corporate.target.com/careers/hiring-process/recorded-video-interviews): HireVue, ~**30 min**, practice question, you may **re-record**; they review within **five business days**. Official interview guide: ~**five minutes** per answer; sample prompts score **job knowledge, inclusivity, problem-solving, connection, drive**. You may apply to **multiple** reqs; same-req reapply after a no is often blocked.

Official does **not** publish a SWE round list. 2026 guides (TechPrep, InterviewQuestions.app) + candidate reports — treat stage *contents* as **reported**.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Official next-step call. Guides: ~30 min; role, hub, why Target, band |
| Recorded video | Official HireVue on **some** business areas (not every Tech req) |
| Early technical | Guides: timed **HackerRank** (2–3 easy–medium + occasional SQL) **or** a live screen |
| Final loop | Guides: pair / **debug-refactor** (SOLID on a messy repo), retail HLD, HM / values |
| Offer | Official Workday letter + pre-hire tasks |

## How this track differs

| vs Walmart / Chewy | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **three values + HireVue + no-live-AI** | Guest + store + .com inventory, not a generic marketplace |
| Pair hour is often **cleanup**, not a blank-editor hard | “I only grind hards silently” misses the values samples |
| Minneapolis / store / FC split is real | Official: you may interview in more than one format |

## Coding and design flavor

Guides: JVM (Java / Kotlin / Spring) plus some Go. Live: medium DSA **or** a buggy project file — they score maintainability. Design: inventory across store vs FC, promotions, checkout peaks, last-mile. Related: [../answers/system-design-payment.md](../answers/system-design-payment.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Target surface (guest app, inventory, FC, store ops) — portable “I like retail” is weak.
2. Official sample: a time you worked with people who think differently (inclusivity).
3. Official sample: how you know the work was impactful (drive).
4. Pair: fix / refactor a messy module; name a SOLID miss before you type.
5. Design: reserve the last unit that is on both Target.com and the aisle.
