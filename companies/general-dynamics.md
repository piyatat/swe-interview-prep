# General Dynamics engineering track

Sits beside [rtx.md](rtx.md) and [lockheed-martin.md](lockheed-martin.md): **ten-business-unit** aerospace / defense — not one loop and not “GDIT-the-integrator” as if Gulfstream or Electric Boat did not exist. Official [FAQs](https://www.gd.com/about-gd/faqs/): Gulfstream, Jet Aviation, Land Systems, European Land Systems, OTS, Bath Iron Works, Electric Boat, NASSCO, **GDIT**, **Mission Systems**. Recruiter confirms **which business + site** (Scottsdale / Dedham GDMS vs Falls Church GDIT vs Groton EB), C++ vs Java, and **clearance**. Corporate HQ **Reston, VA**.

Typical timeline **~2–5 weeks** of active eval (candidate reports); clearance can delay start. Embedded: [../roles/embedded.md](../roles/embedded.md). Behavioral: [../general/behavioral.md](../general/behavioral.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (five-value ethos)

Do **not** invent Amazon LPs. Official [Our Culture](https://www.gd.com/careers/our-culture/) + GDMS [Our Mission](https://gdmissionsystems.com/about-us/our-mission). Map **your** stories.

| Official value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Honesty** | Straight facts; no padded clearance or program claims |
| **Trust** | You owned the outcome when a date slipped |
| **Humanity** | Dignity / safety of people on the program, not a slogan |
| **Alignment** | Your work matches the customer mission, not a side project |
| **Value Creation** | Reuse, less waste, a design you improved on purpose |

“Why GD?” that only says “submarines / I like defense” fails. Name **Mission Systems vs GDIT vs Electric Boat vs Gulfstream** and a **Honesty / Trust** story. Official careers FAQ: each business keeps its **own resume database** — applying to GDMS does **not** enter you at GDIT.

## Official + reported process

Official GDMS [interview preparation](https://gdmissionsystems.com/careers/students-and-recent-graduates/interview-preparation): behavioral, open-ended; bring non-proprietary work samples; veterans prepare 7–9 success stories. They publish sample prompts (accountability, quality, requirements, C++ vs Java, reuse, improve-the-requested-design). Official GDMS [job-seeker FAQ](https://gdmissionsystems.com/careers/faqs): **no AI tools** during interviews (answers, live assist, recording) except approved accessibility. UK Mission Systems FAQ is the same AI-off line and “up to two ~1-hour interviews,” possible technical assessment.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | [gd.com/careers](https://www.gd.com/careers/faq/) **and** the **business** tenant (GDMS / GDIT / EB) |
| Recruiter (reported) | ~15 min: site, salary band, **citizenship / clearance** |
| Interview | Official: behavioral + role knowledge; reported 45–60 min HM or 2–4 panel |
| Coding | **Ask** — some GDMS SWE hours add Valid Parentheses / a snippet; many stay resume + OOP |
| Offer + start | Background / drug screen; clearance can **delay start** |

## How this track differs

| vs RTX / Lockheed | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **five-value ethos**; **ten** BUs, separate ATSs | Loop is often **one or two hours**, not five DSA panels |
| Design is **C4ISR / submarine combat / business-jet avionics**, not a feed | Clearance / ITAR is a **gate** on many reqs |
| “Why GD?” must name **GDMS vs GDIT vs EB vs Gulfstream** | Comp is program bands — not public RSUs |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** GD business — Honesty when you corrected a bad estimate.
2. Official: C++ vs Java; a project you can discuss; worst bug; how much reuse; a design you improved.
3. Pointers in plain language if the JD is C / C++.
4. STAR: feedback you were not meeting expectations (they publish this prompt).
5. Questions: which BU, coding vs panel, live AI (official no at GDMS), hybrid vs site, clearance.
