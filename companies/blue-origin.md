# Blue Origin engineering track

Sits beside [spacex.md](spacex.md) and [lockheed-martin.md](lockheed-martin.md): **New Shepard / New Glenn / Blue Moon** (and TeraWave infra) under **14 published Leadership Principles**, not SpaceX’s no-LP careers page and not a FAANG slate. Official [Careers](https://www.blueorigin.com/careers): **Build the Future. Join Team Blue**; mission is millions of people living and working in space **for the benefit of Earth**. Recruiter confirms **org** (flight software vs engines vs ground / manufacturing vs enterprise), **site** (Kent / Cape / Huntsville / LA / Denver / …), and **US-person / ITAR**.

Typical timeline **~3–6 weeks** (Dataford). Embedded: [../roles/embedded.md](../roles/embedded.md). Debug: [../general/debugging-rounds.md](../general/debugging-rounds.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (14 Leadership Principles)

Do **not** swap in Amazon’s list. Official [Work culture](https://www.blueorigin.com/careers/work-culture) publishes **Blue’s** 14. Map **your** stories; they will not score a recitation.

| Official principle | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Passion for our Mission** | Why **this** vehicle / engine — “space is cool” fails |
| **Customer Focus** | Start with the operator / astronaut / NASA customer |
| **Deliver Results** | Finish through a setback; “let’s figure it out” |
| **Embrace Team Blue** | Dissent, then one direction; feedback you gave |
| **Resourceful** | More with less; constraint as invention |
| **Bias for Action** | Reversible call you made without a 40-page study |
| **Ownership** | Long-term; **never compromise safety**; not “not my job” |
| **Operational Excellence** | Root-cause so the defect stays fixed; mechanisms > intentions |
| **Earn the Trust of Others** | Humility; you changed your mind on evidence |
| **Hire and Develop the Best** | Bar you raised; someone you coached |
| **Insist on the Highest Standards** | Safety front and center; a bar others called unreasonable |
| **Technical Ambition and Simplicity** | Novel architecture **and** a simplification; you pushed back on a req |
| **Practice Humility** | Slow to take credit; asked for help |
| **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** | Challenge, then commit wholly |

Careers tagline on the same family of pages: **Step by Step, Ferociously**. [About Blue](https://www.blueorigin.com/about-blue): Blue Origin means “Earth.”

## Official + reported process

Careers lists openings and sites, **not** a numbered SWE stage list. Dataford (updated 2026-08) + CleverPrep + candidate reports — treat stages as **reported**. **Ask** HackerRank vs presentation-first.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | [Careers](https://www.blueorigin.com/careers) / Workday |
| Recruiter | Mission, org, site, **US citizenship / ITAR**, comp |
| Tech phone (reported) | Resume + CS / language; some loops a timed HackerRank |
| Written (reported, older) | Short essay on human spaceflight — **ask** if your req still wants it |
| Virtual loop (reported) | **30–60 min project presentation** + 3–4 breakouts (coding / design / behavioral); some loops a Bar Raiser |

Live-AI rules are **not** on the careers LP page. Dataford mentions possible AI-assisted coding — **ask**. [../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md](../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md).

| vs SpaceX | vs Lockheed / FAANG |

| Same ITAR gate; different vehicles (**New Glenn / Moon**) | Private company equity — price liquidity separately |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Blue org (New Glenn avionics vs engines vs GSE) — Passion for our Mission.
2. Presentation: 1–2 projects, **your** diffs, a failure, a simplification (Ambition and Simplicity).
3. Coding / debug: parse a stream; do not crash on a short frame.
4. Design: telemetry from a test stand when the link drops 20 s.
5. Questions: org, site, essay vs skip, AI-in-pad, campaign hours.
