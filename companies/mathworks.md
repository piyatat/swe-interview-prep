# MathWorks engineering track

Sits beside [embedded.md](../roles/embedded.md) and [nvidia.md](nvidia.md): **MATLAB / Simulink technical-computing SWE** (Natick HQ + global), not a CUDA kernel loop and not a FAANG feed design. Official mission: accelerate discovery in **engineering and science**. Recruiter confirms **EDG vs product SWE vs web/cloud**, MATLAB vs C++/Java/JS, visa, and **hybrid** (official: ≥3 office days, two synchronous).

Typical path: recruiter or hiring-manager screen → **in-person day that starts with a project presentation** → 4–5 interviews. Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (core values)

Do **not** invent Amazon LPs. Official [Mission and Core Values](https://www.mathworks.com/company/aboutus/mission_values.html). Guiding principle: **do the right thing**. Map **your** stories.

| Official value | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Continuous improvement / excellence** | Right things done well; leave it better; details matter |
| **Rational workplace** | There is a “right answer”; merit; sunlight test; conflict of **ideas**, not people |
| **Credibility and integrity** | Do what you said; whole truth; values over short-term results |
| **Respect and invest** | Golden Rule; coach; leave people better |
| **Learning and self-improvement** | Lifelong learning; everyone leads; get out of the comfort zone |

“Why MathWorks?” that only says “I used MATLAB in school” fails. Name a **numerical, simulation, compiler, or scientific-workflow** problem you have lived. Official: say **I** on your contribution, not only “we.”

## Official process

Official [What to Expect When You Apply](https://www.mathworks.com/company/jobs/resources/applying-and-interviewing.html). EDG campus flavor: [Interviewing at MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/company/jobs/students/interviewing.html) (US/UK EDG).

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | Careers account; English CV required; referrals are a **named** best source |
| First interview | Phone or virtual with **recruiter or hiring manager** |
| On-site day | Starts with presenting **background + a project of your choice** to the team, then individual interviews |
| Who | Hiring manager, recruiter, a few people tied to the role; extra folks may join **presentation only** |
| Length | Four or five interviewers; most of a day; lunch break; virtual may split across days |
| Technical test | HM often screens by phone/video; you **may** get live coding in a shared editor |
| After | Typical feedback within **two days** of the formal round |
| References | **Three** professional references **before** offer (managers; new grads often professors) |
| AI in hiring | Official: recruiting team does **not** use AI tools for evaluation / hiring decisions |

EDG prep (official): problem-solving, programming + engineering concepts, writing/debugging/discussing code, communication. Languages they list: MATLAB, Python, C++, Java, C, JavaScript. Domain if relevant: controls, signal processing, embedded. Practice: clean code (they name HackerRank) **or** MATLAB Onramp.

Reports (campus): HireVue + OA with coding **or** MATLAB MCQ — **ask**. Dress: official **business casual**.

## How this track differs

| vs NVIDIA / embedded product | vs generic FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Product is **technical computing** (MATLAB/Simulink + 100+ toolboxes) | Day **opens with your talk**, not a silent coding pad |
| Values include **rational workplace** and design reviews | Official: **no AI in hiring decisions** |
| EDG is ~50/50 engineers and CS (official fun fact) | Three references are a **hard** last step |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** scientific-computing company — a user workflow you would make faster, not “I like MATLAB.”
2. Presentation: one project, **your** decisions, a miss, what you would change; slides optional unless they ask.
3. Live coding: clean fundamentals; narrate; they may mix CS with an engineering story.
4. Values: a time you changed your mind because the **better argument** won (rational workplace).
5. Questions: EDG vs product team, MATLAB bar, hybrid days, visa, AI-in-pad (distinct from “AI in hiring”).
