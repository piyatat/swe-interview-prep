# Red Hat engineering track

Sits beside [ibm.md](ibm.md) and [hashicorp.md](hashicorp.md): **open-source enterprise (RHEL, OpenShift, Ansible, Fedora / CentOS Stream)**, IBM-owned, not a consumer social loop and not HashiCorp’s official principles-hire Go / infra day. Official [hiring process](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs/info-guide) + [AI usage guidelines for candidates](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs/ai-usage-guidelines). Recruiter confirms **product vs consulting vs support**, **Java / Python / Go / C**, hybrid, and **whether your technical hour allows Copilot-class tools**.

Typical official path: **apply → resume review → preliminary phone (may include aptitude) → business/technical 1:1 → optional case/presentation → competency panel → offer**. Most interviews are **virtual** unless they say otherwise; they coordinate and cover intercity / interstate travel for onsites. Culture: [../general/behavioral.md](../general/behavioral.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (four values)

Do **not** invent extra pillars. Official [Our culture](https://www.redhat.com/en/about/our-culture): open-source principles (freedom, community, sustainability) inform business values.

| Official value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Freedom to contribute** | You shipped or upstreamed something; ideas from anywhere |
| **Courage to express thoughts and opinions** | Respectful dissent; you said the unpopular technical no |
| **Commitment to see things through** | Finished the hard part; supported the community after merge |
| **Accountability to customers, community, and each other** | Named owner; customer + upstream, not only the ticket |

Official hire page: creative problem solvers who believe you do more by **working together openly**. “Why Red Hat?” that only says “Linux / IBM is stable” fails — name an **upstream, support-lifecycle, or hybrid-cloud** problem you have lived.

## Official AI policy (do not guess)

Official [candidate AI guidelines](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs/ai-usage-guidelines). They want the real you **and** treat AI as a normal workplace tool.

| Stage | Official allowed | Official not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application | Refine / personalize a **self-written** CV or letter | Entirely generate application materials |
| Prep | Research, mocks, interview prep | Rely **solely** on AI scripts for live answers |
| Business / panel | **Unassisted only** — no AI tools of any kind | Copilot / notes / hidden model in the room |
| Technical | **Some teams** allow AI-assisted coding (e.g. Copilot) **if** the interviewer says yes; **disclose tool + how it helps up front** | Use AI tooling without asking |
| Red Hat’s own AI | Scheduling / reminders only | Official: **AI is not used** to decide your application or interview; humans evaluate |

If the interviewer has not said AI is allowed in the technical hour, treat it as **off**.

## Official process

Official [hiring process](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs/info-guide).

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Application / referral | Careers site or employee referral link; Talent Community if no req |
| Resume review | Talent Acquisition; phone if you fit |
| Preliminary phone | Interest + qualifications; may include **technical, language, or analytical** assessments |
| Business / technical | Usually **1:1** with HM or teammate; some technicals are **in a Red Hat office** |
| Case / technical presentation (some roles) | Present a topic or project to a **panel**; methods, findings, questions |
| Panel | Competency **behavioral**: can you do the job, and will you add to the culture? Complex-problem approach + recent situations |
| Offer / no | Terms and start date, or a phone/email no; resume kept for later reqs |

Official fraud note: scammers pose as recruiters on social. Confirm `@redhat.com` (or the address your recruiter names).

## How this track differs

| vs IBM / HashiCorp | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **written** candidate AI rules; panel after the technical | Open-org values, not Leadership Principles |
| Optional **presentation** hour | Upstream + enterprise lifecycle stories matter |
| IBM-owned; still a Red Hat loop | “I only grind Blind 75” misses the panel |

## Coding and design flavor

SWE work is product + community: packaging, operators, Ansible content, build/test, supportable upgrades. Live: practical coding or a project walk, not olympiad graphs. Design: multi-tenant OpenShift, image/signing, what happens when an upgrade must be reversible. Related: [../roles/devops-sre.md](../roles/devops-sre.md), [../roles/platform.md](../roles/platform.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Red Hat product (RHEL, OpenShift, Ansible, …) — portable “I like Linux” is weak.
2. Technical: debug or extend existing code; ask AI policy **before** you open an assistant.
3. Presentation (if scheduled): a project with methods, findings, and what you would redo.
4. Panel: courage (you disagreed) or accountability (customer + community).
5. Questions: office vs virtual, presentation hour, Copilot, which BU.
