# Cadence Design Systems engineering track

Sits beside [synopsys.md](synopsys.md) and [asml.md](asml.md): **EDA / intelligent-system design software** (digital implementation, analog, verification, IP, molecular science), not a consumer SoC loop and not Temporal’s workflow engine. Official [Careers](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/life-at-cadence/careers.html): software developers **architect and optimize the tools** that enable complete electronic designs, from chips to systems. Recruiter confirms **product SWE vs applications / product engineer vs IP**, C++ vs Python, site (San Jose, Austin, Noida, …), and AI-in-pad.

Typical path (official recruiter blog): recruiter phone screen → one or two phone interviews (may include a coding test or homework) → one or two on-site rounds. Candidates normally meet **six to eight** Cadence employees. Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture

Do **not** invent Amazon LPs. Official [Life at Cadence](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/life-at-cadence.html). Map **your** stories.

| Official value | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Innovation — Think Big** | Computational software for chips, boards, and systems |
| **Quality — Deliver Excellence** | Designers tape out on this code; correctness is the product |
| **Agility — Move Quickly** | Node, customer, and AI-for-design schedules move |
| **Integrity — Be Accountable** | Own the module; say what is not ready |
| **One Cadence — One Team** | Cross-group collaboration; trust and respect |
| **Customers — Their Success is Our Success** | Why Cadence exists |

“Why Cadence?” that only says “semiconductors / Bay Area” fails. Name a **graph, geometry, compiler, or verification** problem you have lived — they write the software **other** chip teams run. Early career: official [Interns and New Grads](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/careers/interns-and-new-grads.html) + Career Catalyst Program.

## Official process

Official [How to Land a Job at Cadence](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/life-at-cadence/posts/how-to-land-a-job-at-cadence) (Talent Acquisition). Stages **vary by group**.

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | Careers page; tailor resume to the **top three** req skills |
| Recruiter screen | Phone; role / site / product family |
| Phone interviews | One or two; may include a **coding test or homework** |
| On-site | One or two rounds; **six to eight** Cadence employees across the loop |
| Early career | SWE, EDA SWE, IC / physical design, or applications / product engineer |

Reports (Dataford 2026; campus GFG): C++ memory / vtable / smart pointers / move; hash tables; OA of C++/Java MCQs + two mediums, sometimes a **review of your OA answers**. Treat puzzle lists and round **count** as **reported**. Pair: [../general/cs-fundamentals.md](../general/cs-fundamentals.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## How this track differs

| vs Synopsys EDA | vs NVIDIA / Qualcomm product silicon |
| --- | --- |
| Official **One Team** six values, not ACE+T | Product is **tools + IP**, not a GPU or modem |
| Recruiter-published loop + homework option | Graphs / geometry / STA show up more than social-feed design |
| Molecular-science SWE exists beside EDA | C++ memory + RAII are baseline on many teams |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** EDA house — a place-and-route, timing, or verification constraint, not “I like chips.”
2. C++: stack vs heap, virtual destructor, unique vs shared ptr, a segfault you chased.
3. Hash table with collisions, or cycle in a netlist — [../answers/coding-course-schedule.md](../answers/coding-course-schedule.md).
4. One Team: a time you aligned software with hardware/PD when the spec moved.
5. Questions: product vs applications, C++ vs Python, homework vs live, AI-in-pad.
