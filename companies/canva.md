# Canva engineering track

Sits beside [figma.md](figma.md) and [adobe.md](adobe.md): **visual editor + templates + Magic Studio**, not a generic FAANG DSA slate. Official [How we hire](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/how-we-hire/): hiring is a **two-way** conversation; recruiters write from `@canva.com` (personal Gmail claiming Canva is fraud). Recruiter confirms **backend vs frontend vs ML**, **AI-assisted coding tool** (Cursor / Copilot / Claude), hub (Sydney, other), and the **3-hour** final.

Typical timeline **4–5 weeks** (official How we hire). AI hour: [../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md](../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official Skills Hiring Framework (do not invent extra pillars)

Official How we hire — these four **structure interview questions**. Ask the recruiter how they apply to **your** loop.

| Official pillar | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Craft** | Specialty skill; effective engineer, not a title |
| **Strategy** | Pick the path that leaps the company; many valid approaches |
| **Communication** | Alignment across team and company |
| **Leadership & Coaching** | Empower others; critical if you lead projects |

Official behavioral format is **STARR** (Situation, Task, Action, Results **and Reflection**) — not STAR-only.

Canva Team culture write-up (six values; do not treat as a secret LP list): **Be a force for good**, **Empower others**, **Pursue excellence**, **Be a good human**, **Make complex things simple**, **Set crazy big goals and make them happen**.

“Why Canva?” that only says “I use the product / they are Australian” fails. Name a **template, editor, or AI-design** problem you have lived.

## Official AI policy (they insist)

Official [engineering blog](https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/yes-you-can-use-ai-in-our-interviews/) (2025-06-11) + How we hire FAQ:

| Role | Official rule |
| --- | --- |
| Backend / frontend / ML | **Expect** Copilot, Cursor, or Claude on the technical hour. CS-fundamentals screen was **replaced** by **AI-Assisted Coding** |
| Other roles | Encouraged to use AI; be ready to say what you kept / changed |
| Everyone | Original thinking; interview may be **AI-recorded** (opt-out does not hurt) |

They redesigned prompts to be **ambiguous and realistic** because classic DSA is solved in seconds. Score: clarify, chunk work, **review** generated code, production bar. Recruiter tells you the tool and whether the hour is live screen-share.

## Official + reported process

Official How we hire — four components; **engineers skip the take-home**.

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | [Life at Canva](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/); 1–2 weeks to first update |
| Round 1 — Zoom (TA) | Aspirations + culture; **engineering: technical questions on this call** |
| Round 2 — first interview | Coach or close peer; **engineering: peer problem-solving** |
| Round 3 | Non-eng: take-home. **Engineers: no take-home** — recruiter names the substitute (guides: the AI-assisted hour) |
| Round 4 — final | Official: **~3 hours**, technical problem-solving + experience / Skills Framework |
| Decision | Official: generally **4–5 weeks** recruiter → outcome |

Guides (2026: FinalRound AI, TechPrep) — treat composition as **reported**: AI-assisted product coding (~60 min) + design / language fluency + behavioral inside the final block.

## How this track differs

| vs Figma / Adobe | vs Meta / Google AI hour |
| --- | --- |
| Official **insist-on-AI** for BE/FE/ML | Meta/Google keep a **classic no-AI** DSA hour beside the AI slot |
| Official **no take-home** for engineers | Design is **editor / templates / export**, not “design Twitter” |
| STARR + Skills Framework | “I never use Copilot” is a **failing** story here |

## Coding and design flavor

The AI hour is a **product slice** (scaffold + ambiguous brief), not two mediums. You own every line the model writes. Final loop may still hit language fluency (JVM, React, Python) and a **Canva-shaped** design: templates, fonts, export jobs, collab. Related: [../answers/system-design-collab-editor.md](../answers/system-design-collab-editor.md), [../general/code-review-rounds.md](../general/code-review-rounds.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Canva group (editor, growth, ML / Magic) — portable “I like design tools” is weak.
2. AI hour: clarify the airport / template brief; prompt a **subtask**; reject a sloppy diff.
3. Design: template publish + render farm; what is sync vs async.
4. STARR: you simplified a messy UX or coached someone through a bad AI patch.
5. Questions for them: hybrid, coach, how Craft is leveled.
