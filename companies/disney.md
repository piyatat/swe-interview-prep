# Disney / Walt Disney Company engineering track

Sits beside [ea.md](ea.md) and [netflix.md](netflix.md): **media + parks + streaming (Disney+ / Hulu / ESPN+)**, not an EA studio/engine loop and not Netflix’s keeper-test memo. Official [careers](https://www.disneycareers.com/en/working-here/our-culture): the company **entertains, informs and inspires** through storytelling. Recruiter confirms **Disney Entertainment vs ESPN vs Parks vs ILM / Pixar**, stack, hub, and whether the first hour is live coding or a take-home.

Typical reported path **~2–6 weeks** (2026 guides). Coding: [../general/coding-patterns.md](../general/coding-patterns.md). Behavioral: [../general/behavioral.md](../general/behavioral.md). Streaming design: [../answers/system-design-video-streaming.md](../answers/system-design-video-streaming.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (story + guest)

Do **not** invent extra “Disney Leadership Principles.” Official candidate pages hire against **your story** and **guest** impact. Map **your** stories.

| Official line | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Mission** | Entertain, inform, and inspire through unparalleled storytelling |
| **People at the heart** | Employees and Cast Members fuel the business and the guest experience |
| **Inclusion** | Belong and thrive; welcoming, respectful environment for employees **and** guests |
| **STAR (official)** | Situation / Task / Action / Result — you are the **leading character** |

“Why Disney?” that only says “I grew up on the movies / I like streaming” fails. Name a **guest, catalog, park-ops, or playback** problem you have lived.

Official [Candidate AI Usage](https://www.disneycareers.com/en/ai-usage): prep (research, resume polish, practice questions) is welcome. **Do not** use AI to record / transcribe interviews, fabricate experience, complete assessments unless they say so, generate live answers, or use deepfakes / voice clones. Humans make hiring decisions. Official recruiters email only `@disney.com`, `@espn.com`, `@lucasfilm.com`, `@ilm.com`, `@pixar.com`, or `@abc.com`.

## Official process

Official [Candidate Resources](https://www.disneycareers.com/en/candidate-resources) + [interview prep](https://www.disneycareers.com/en/interview-prep) (2026):

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | Recruiter review of the submission |
| Screen | Recruiter conversation if skills align |
| Interview(s) | Hiring-team conversations; official pages emphasize **behavioral STAR** |
| Selection | Hiring team decides; you hear yes or no |

Official does **not** publish a universal SWE round list. 2026 guides (TechPrep) — treat stage *contents* as **reported**: recruiter (~20–30 min), sometimes a deeper HR / hiring-partner culture hour, live coding **or** take-home, then a 3–4 hour virtual loop (coding, design, behavioral / cross-functional).

## How this track differs

| vs EA / Netflix | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **STAR + guest language**; AI-off unless invited | Design is **catalog / parks / playback**, not a social feed |
| Segment (ESPN vs Parks vs Studio) is a real split | “I only grind hards silently” misses the story hour |
| Official: humans decide; AI may draft JDs / schedule | Cross-functional panel can include a non-engineer |

## Coding and design flavor

Guides: medium DSA (arrays / trees / BFS) with **readable** code over golf. Streaming / ESPN+ roles raise the bar on p50/p95 startup, rebuffer, concurrency. Related: [../answers/system-design-ticketmaster.md](../answers/system-design-ticketmaster.md), [../answers/system-design-chat.md](../answers/system-design-chat.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Disney surface (Disney+, ESPN, Parks, studio tools) — portable “I like media” is weak.
2. Official STAR: a time you resolved a conflict or learned from a miss (you drove the Action).
3. Coding: grid / tree BFS; narrate tradeoffs after the first pass.
4. Design: Continue Watching or a park reservation hold — consistency vs guest-visible latency.
5. Guest: a backend choice you can explain to a Product Owner in plain language.
