# Citadel LLC engineering track

Sits beside [citadel-securities.md](citadel-securities.md) and [quant.md](quant.md): **multi-strategy investment SWE** (research platforms, risk, market data, ops / finance systems) — not the Securities **one-problem four-stage** market-making day and not Jane Street’s official open-ended pair loop. Official [Engineering](https://www.citadel.com/careers/engineering/): engineers are **embedded with researchers and investment professionals**; impact is commercial. Recruiter confirms **Citadel vs Citadel Securities**, **SWE vs quant developer vs UI vs SRE**, language, city, and AI.

Typical path: campus **~eight weeks, dual-firm**; experienced mix of **coding + system design + problem-solving** (FAQs, Aug 2026). Pair / LLD: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (five values)

Do **not** invent Amazon LPs. Official [Our Culture](https://www.citadel.com/who-we-are/our-culture/): **the best idea wins**, regardless of title. Map **your** stories. Securities adds **Mutuality** as a sixth line — do not mix the tables.

| Official value | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Integrity** | Direct, accountable; compliance is not optional |
| **Winning** | Hard problems others walk away from |
| **Meritocracy** | Career and pay on delivered results, not tenure |
| **Learning** | New ideas that strengthen the edge |
| **Extraordinary Colleagues** | Collaboration; more together than solo |

“Why Citadel?” that only says “hedge fund / I like C++” fails. Name a **research-platform, risk, data, or incomplete-spec** problem you have lived. Official FAQs: engineers are **strategic partners**, not a ticket queue; success is **impact** (quality + reliability + business value). **In-office** culture with flexibility; performance over face time.

## Official process — campus (dual-firm)

Official [Campus Engineering Interview Process](https://www.citadel.com/careers/career-perspectives/our-engineering-interview-process/) (~eight weeks). **Citadel and Citadel Securities in one process** — ask who is in the room.

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| First round | **45 min** video; coding + DS/algo + behavioral; interests, internships, why Citadel |
| Second round | Usually **three 45s**; tech + behavioral; next steps within **two weeks** |
| Leadership | Senior engineer **for a specific team** if a manager wants you (can be multiple) |
| Final review | Team fit; if several teams, recruiter helps you choose |

Official: no single profile; they want real-world problems, collaboration, **commercial outcomes**, and judgment at speed. Practical, not trick questions. Assess before coding; ask; name tradeoffs; say what you do not know; take hints. Competing offer — **tell them**. One role type at a time (eng vs quant). Interviews Mon–Fri business hours.

## Official process — experienced (Citadel LLC)

Official [FAQs: Engineering at Citadel](https://www.citadel.com/careers/career-perspectives/faqs-engineering-at-citadel/) (16 Aug 2026). **Not** the Securities 4–5 hour one-problem day unless the recruiter says you are on **that** firm.

| Official line | What to prep |
| --- | --- |
| Mix of **coding, system design, problem-solving** | Fundamentals + scalable systems + how you iterate |
| Language-**agnostic** interviews (some reqs lock a stack) | Day-to-day: **Python, Java, C++**; pick the language you are fastest in |
| AI / coding agents used on the job | Comfort in evolving tools; **ask** live-AI rules for the pad |
| Design flavor | Research frameworks, risk, **billions of market-data values**, order flow — not a social feed |

Official: less memorization, more breakdown / tradeoffs / iteration. Roles on the engineering page: Software Engineer, Quantitative Developer (QR engineer), UI Engineer, SRE.

## How this track differs

| vs Citadel Securities | vs Jane Street / FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Same **five** values minus Mutuality; campus loop is **shared** | Design is **investment infra / risk / research**, not a feed |
| Experienced LLC is **coding + HLD**, not four stages on one matching problem | “I only grind Blind 75” misses commercial impact + incomplete spec |
| Ask **which entity** on every email | In-office; titles matter less than problem scope |

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Citadel LLC seat (research platform vs risk vs data vs corporate tech) — “I want a hedge fund” is weak.
2. Screen: medium coding; narrate tests; say what you would cut.
3. Design: a research backtest / market-data fan-out / risk snapshot under a new SLO.
4. Values: a time the **best idea was not yours** (meritocracy / colleagues).
5. Questions: which firm, C++ lock, live AI, city, campus vs experienced slate.
