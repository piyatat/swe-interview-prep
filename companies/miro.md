# Miro engineering track

Sits beside [figma.md](figma.md) and [notion.md](notion.md): **infinite collaboration canvas** (workshops, diagrams, hybrid rituals), not a pixel-design tool and not a docs block store. Official [Engineering](https://miro.com/careers/teams/engineering/): 100M+ users; vision is **collective, never independent**. Recruiter confirms **hub** (Amsterdam, Berlin, Yerevan, …), IC vs EM track, and AI-in-pad.

## Official hire bar (do not invent extra pillars)

Official [values](https://miro.com/careers/life-at-miro/culture/company-values/) — co-designed by Mironeers. Map **your** stories.

| Official value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Play as a team to win the world** | Cross-stream, not a solo hero; celebrate as a team |
| **Practice empathy to gain insight** | Customer + teammate perspective; multicultural hubs |
| **Focus on impact and make it happen** | Prioritize outcomes; own the commitment |
| **Learn, grow, and drive change** | Open reflection on success **and** failure |

Official recruiter write-up: Behaviors hour leans on **collaboration** and **iteration**. Do not invent extra LP names.

“Why Miro?” that only says “I use the whiteboard” fails. Name a **multiplayer canvas, workshop, or hybrid-ritual** problem you have lived.

## Official process (Engineering page)

Official [How we hire](https://miro.com/careers/teams/engineering/) is a **five-stage** SWE loop:

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Recruiter screen | ~30 min: role, background, **why Miro** |
| 02 Live coding | Algorithmic; problem-solving + communication. Recruiter article: ~1 h, **≤2** problems, two engineers |
| 03 Systems design | Architecture + tradeoffs. Recruiter article: **on a Miro board** — learn the product first |
| 04 Miro Behaviors | Peers; evidence-based questions vs values + behaviors |
| 05 Leadership round | Leadership; values + your vision of success at Miro |

Older recruiter article (2022): Zoom; decision **within 24 hours** after HM. Confirm **your** loop and AI-in-pad — the numbered list is current; the 24-hour SLA may have moved.

## How this track differs

| vs Figma / Notion | vs FAANG web |
| --- | --- |
| Official **five stages**; design hour is **on Miro** | Design is **canvas / presence / workshop scale**, not “design Twitter” |
| Values hour is **Behaviors + Leadership**, not a G&L script | Silent CoderPad with no board fluency is the wrong sport |
| Hybrid **Miro Together** hubs | Ask languages + AI; do not assume an AI-collaborative hour |

## Coding and design flavor

Official streams: Canvas & Collaboration, Core Platform, Infra & Data, Growth, Security. Live work looks like graphs / intervals / undo, then a board: viewport culling, presence, CRDT/OT vs OT, or workshop-scale objects. Related: [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md), [../roles/frontend.md](../roles/frontend.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** collaboration canvas — portable “I like Figma” is weak.
2. Live: merge intervals / undo stack / hit-test; then two clients edit.
3. Design on a **Miro board**: presence cursors, disconnect, 40 viewers.
4. Value: Play as a team — you unblocked another stream instead of shipping solo.
5. Questions for them: languages, AI-in-pad, hub days, Behaviors vs Leadership split.
