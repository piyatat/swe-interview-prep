# Wix engineering track

Sits beside [webflow.md](webflow.md) and [canva.md](canva.md): **create-the-web-for-all site builder (ADI / Editor / business suite)**, not Webflow’s official Codeflow hour and not Canva’s insisted AI-assisted coding loop. Official [How we hire](https://careers.wix.com/how-we-hire): **hire people, not resumes**; mission is **create the web for all**. Recruiter confirms **frontend vs backend vs infra / Editor vs business products**, office vs hybrid vs remote, and the **home assignment**.

Typical official target is **about one month**. Take-homes: [../general/take-homes.md](../general/take-homes.md). CS: [../general/cs-fundamentals.md](../general/cs-fundamentals.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official culture (How we hire)

Do **not** invent extra pillars. Official [How we hire](https://careers.wix.com/how-we-hire) publishes four culture lines plus a hire bar (skills over titles; own path; motivation / potential / values; diverse perspectives). Map **your** stories.

| Official line | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Driven by doing good** | Democratize the web; tools so anyone can succeed online |
| **Prioritize your development** | DoubleYou + on-the-job training for juniors / students |
| **Encourage internal mobility** | Their own mobility platform; apply next door |
| **Celebrate who you are** | Mosaic of beliefs, orientations, backgrounds |
| **Skills over titles** | No fancy-school or formal-degree requirement |
| **Motivation, potential, values** | Translate a different background into what Wix needs |

“Why Wix?” that only says “no-code / Israel / I used the editor” fails. Name an **editor, template, or hosted-site** problem you have lived.

## Official process

Official [How we hire](https://careers.wix.com/how-we-hire) is **six** numbered stages. Official FAQ: they try to finish in **one month**; you may apply to several roles but **interview for only one at a time**; they will reroute you if another req fits; salary is usually at offer (some locations discuss range earlier).

| Stage | Official |
| --- | --- |
| Apply | They review every CV; if a fit, they outline next steps |
| First professional interview | Short call **or** ~60 min; skills / goals; they may suggest another role |
| Home assignment | **Short**; official equalizer for people who do not shine live; they say they try to respect your time |
| Second professional interview | Team Lead: assignment walkthrough, background, day-to-day, team fit |
| HR interview | In-depth (sometimes more than one): aspirations, interests; **you** should leave knowing Wix |
| Decision & offer | Best candidate gets the offer + benefits; then start date |

Official work model: **office together is the default** (creativity / collaboration); hybrid and remote-only exist **by team and location** — ask. English: high written + spoken, not native. Degree: **not** required.

### What the hours actually test (official + employee)

Official assignment is a **common-problem** slice of the work, not a weekend product. Official [myth-busting](https://careers.wix.com/post/interviewing-at-wix-myth-busting-time) (Frontend Guild, 2024; still the careers explainer): a backend hire reports the live technical hour was **CS fundamentals + an algorithm + engineering questions**, **not** Java / JVM trivia; HR is the **values / attitude** closer. Another hire: **no trial period** — the loop is the filter. Treat round *counts* (4 vs 5) as **team-reported**.

## How this track differs

| vs Webflow / Canva | vs FAANG |
| --- | --- |
| Official **home assignment + Team Lead debrief + HR closer** | Site-builder scale (ADI / Editor), not a feed |
| Skills-over-titles; they will **reroute** roles | “I only grind hards silently” misses the assignment talk |
| Office-first unless the req says otherwise | Official: finish in ~a month, not a six-hour Superday |

## Coding and design flavor

Official product: Editor, templates, hosting, business suite. Live CS hour (employee report): algorithms + how systems fit — they onboard Scala / stack later. Design: multi-tenant sites, editor state, publish / rollback, A/B. Related: [../answers/system-design-collab-editor.md](../answers/system-design-collab-editor.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** Wix surface (Editor, ADI, business app) — portable “I like no-code” is weak.
2. First professional: a system you would still defend; they may pitch another req.
3. Home assignment: finish a short, readable slice; note tradeoffs for the TL hour.
4. Team Lead: walk the assignment, then how you would extend it.
5. HR: motivation + how you treat people — official closer, not a formality.
