# Shopify engineering track

Sits beside [product-companies.md](product-companies.md) and [startup.md](startup.md): **commerce platform + Life Story + craft**, not a generic FAANG slate. Official [Careers](https://www.shopify.com/careers/): mission **make commerce better for everyone**; work is **Digital by Design** (ship from anywhere; Ports / Bursts / Summit for IRL). Official Product / intern process pages name **Life Story** as a scored stage. Recruiter is the source of truth for **craft assessment vs pair**, **AI policy**, and team.

Typical timeline **3–5 weeks** (guides). Confirm **level** and whether R&D skips the first call into pair. Coding: [../general/pair-programming.md](../general/pair-programming.md). AI-allowed hour: [../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md](../general/ai-assisted-rounds.md). Checkout / money design: [../answers/system-design-payment.md](../answers/system-design-payment.md).

## Official culture (Careers)

Do **not** recite the table. Map **your** stories to the behaviors.

| Official line | Careers one-liner |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept our mission** | Commerce as free speech; care about merchants you may not agree with |
| **Thrive on change** | Week-not-quarter pace; resilient in ambiguity |
| **Chase excellence** | Growth is an expectation; work with driven crafters |
| **Digital by Design** | Trust over location; ship where you work best |
| **Everyone works on product** | Product thinking + **reflexive AI** as a differentiator |

CEO Tobi Lütke’s public 2025 memo (posted on X after a leak): **reflexive AI usage is a baseline expectation** — use it in craft, including interviews when invited. Verify output; do not paste a canned solution.

## Official + reported process

| Stage | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Apply / recruiter** | Fit, level, logistics — [Careers](https://www.shopify.com/careers/) |
| **Craft assessment** (official on discipline pages) | Role-specific bar; R&D often **pair-programs** |
| **Life Story** (official) | Themes of the journey, what you learned, how you made a difference — **not** a STAR quiz |
| Pair / craft interviews (guides) | Incremental build, tests, collaboration; docs + AI often **allowed** |
| Project / HM / team placement | Ownership depth; merchant-minded tradeoffs; questions you ask back |

Guides (2026): pair is **75–90 min**, you drive, start something that **runs** in the first ten minutes. Life Story is chronological **why** behind pivots — resume recitation and canned STAR both fail.

## How this track differs

| vs FAANG | vs Stripe / Uber (product-company bucket) |
| --- | --- |
| **Life Story** is a named gate for everyone | Stripe is integration / bug-bash; Uber is CodeSignal + graphs |
| Pair + AI is the **job**, not a forbidden overlay | Merchant checkout / inventory / flash-sale constraints |
| Digital-by-design remote + occasional Bursts | Generalist hire → **team placement** after craft |

## Coding and design flavor

Pair problems look like a **CLI cart, inventory, or parser** — grow features and tests, not one clever trick. Design: checkout conversion, flash-sale reads, inventory correctness, webhook retries. Related: [../answers/system-design-notification.md](../answers/system-design-notification.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. “Walk me through your path — why each move, including a bet that failed.”
2. Pair: cart + tax + a late “gift cards expire” rule; keep tests green.
3. “You used the assistant here — what did you verify before keeping it?”
4. Design checkout for a flash sale: what degrades first, what must not.
5. Project deep-dive: hardest call, metric, what you would change.

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Read [Careers](https://www.shopify.com/careers/) + one discipline page (Life Story + craft)
- [ ] Recruiter: pair vs take-home, AI tools, Life Story length, team placement
- [ ] Timeline map: 3–4 real pivots with **reasons**, said aloud
- [ ] One **90-min pair** of a messy small app with AI **on**, narrating
- [ ] One merchant-systems design mock ([../general/system-design.md](../general/system-design.md))
- [ ] Comp after written offer (Flex Comp mix is reported): [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md)
