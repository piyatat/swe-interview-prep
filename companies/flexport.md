# Flexport engineering track

Sits beside [samsara.md](samsara.md), [booking.md](booking.md), and [uber.md](uber.md): **digital freight / global trade OS**, not fleet IoT hardware and not a consumer marketplace loop. Official [Interview Prep](https://interviewing.flexport.com/): “make global commerce so easy there will be more of it”; phone/fax/spreadsheet freight is the problem. Recruiter confirms **tracking / customs vs marketplace vs payments vs platform**, Ruby/Java/Go vs TypeScript, and CoderPad vs OA.

Typical timeline **~2–4 weeks** (2026 guides). OA: [../general/online-assessments.md](../general/online-assessments.md). Event ingest cousin: [../answers/system-design-notification.md](../answers/system-design-notification.md). Comp: [../general/offer-negotiation.md](../general/offer-negotiation.md).

## Official hire bar (do not invent extra pillars)

Official [About Flexport](https://interviewing.flexport.com/about-flexport) lists **five** values. Map **your** stories.

| Official value | What they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Empower the Client** | Customer obsession; they win only when the shipper / broker wins |
| **Simplify** | Cut steps, assumptions, and jargon; scalable clarity |
| **Win As A Team** | Cross-office / cross-function; no hierarchy-as-blocker |
| **Play the Long Game** | Safety, compliance, trust over a short-term booking |
| **Act Like An Entrepreneur** | Curious, status-quo-unsatisfied, principled change |

Official [Interview Logistics](https://interviewing.flexport.com/interview-logistics): interviews are **conversational**; questions are **behavioral** (STAR) and **situational**; they hire people who **add** to culture, not just fit. Review the five values before the loop.

“Why Flexport?” that only says “logistics / I like supply chain” fails. Name a **late event, customs exception, or multi-party booking** problem you have lived.

## Official + reported process

Official logistics (not a published stage list):

| Stage | Official / reported |
| --- | --- |
| Kickoff | Often a phone call; then **Google Meet** (NA/EU) or **Zoom** (Asia) |
| Conversations | Behavioral + situational; time for **your** questions |
| Final | May be **onsite** so you meet the team; recruiter sends the invite |
| Feedback | They try; not guaranteed at every stage |

2026 Dataford / PracHub / knok — treat extra stages as **reported** (confirm with recruiter): recruiter or OA → ~60-min CoderPad (live coding or domain modeling) → virtual onsite (practical OOD, logistics-flavored design, project deep dive, EM behavioral). Guides: ~2–4 weeks. Confirm AI-in-pad; official pages do **not** publish a live-tool permit.

## How this track differs

| vs Samsara / Booking / Uber | vs FAANG web |
| --- | --- |
| Official **five values** on the candidate site | Design is **shipment + document + exception**, not “design Twitter” |
| Physical cargo, customs, and third-party carriers | “I only grind LeetCode” misses Simplify + client stories |
| Multi-party (shipper, forwarder, broker, carrier) | Ask AI-in-pad; do not assume an AI hour |

## Coding and design flavor

Guides: incremental OOD (status machines), graphs / intervals / grids, event ingest with **out-of-order** timestamps. Design: tracking, reservation concurrency, idempotent carrier webhooks. Related: [../answers/coding-meeting-rooms.md](../answers/coding-meeting-rooms.md), [../general/low-level-design.md](../general/low-level-design.md).

## Sample prompts (shapes, not leaked puzzles)

1. Why **this** freight OS — portable “supply chain” is weak.
2. Live: merge overlapping transit windows, or model a shipment status machine.
3. Design: container tracking events that arrive late / twice — what you store vs drop.
4. Value: Simplify — a time you deleted a step the customer never needed.
5. Questions for them: CoderPad vs OA, onsite final, languages, AI-in-pad.
