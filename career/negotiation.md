# Negotiation

Negotiate **after** you have (or expect) a written offer. Goal: improve the package and level while keeping the relationship intact.

**Disclaimer:** Any dollar figures you or an agent mention are **estimates**, not guarantees. Prefer ranges, levers, and public bands (e.g. Levels.fyi) over invented precision.

## Comp levers (typical priority)

1. **Level / title** — often moves everything else  
2. **Base salary**  
3. **Equity** (refresh, strike/grant size, vesting)  
4. **Sign-on** (cash bridge for unvested equity left behind)  
5. **Bonus target**  
6. **Start date, remote, relocation, learning budget**  

Startups may trade cash for equity or vice versa — clarify runway and dilution assumptions at a high level ([offers.md](offers.md)).

## Timing

| Moment | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Share **range** if pressed; anchor to market + level, not desperation |
| Mid-loop | Focus on fit; don’t hard-negotiate yet |
| Verbal offer | Thank them; ask for **written** details + deadline |
| Written offer | Negotiate with data + alternatives |

## Talking points pattern

1. Enthusiasm for the team/problem  
2. One concrete ask (level or base first)  
3. Evidence: market band for level, competing timeline (if real), unique scope you bring  
4. Collaborative close: “What flexibility exists on X?”  

### Example (base / level)

> I’m excited about this role and the team. Based on the scope we discussed and market bands for [level], I was targeting closer to [range]. Is there flexibility on level or base to get there?

### Example (sign-on)

> I have unvested equity that I’d forfeit. A sign-on of [range] would make the transition viable while keeping the ongoing package as proposed.

## Rules of thumb

- Negotiate from **interest**, not ultimatums  
- One coordinated ask beats drip-feed requests  
- Never invent competing offers  
- Get final terms **in writing**  
- Know your walk-away ([offers.md](offers.md))  

## Checklist

- [ ] Written offer + total comp sketch  
- [ ] Public market context for level (labeled estimate)  
- [ ] Ranked levers and walk-away  
- [ ] Single clear ask ready  
- [ ] Deadline / decision date known
