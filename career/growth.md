# Growth and promotion

After you land the role, compound **skill + scope + visibility**. Promotion is usually evidence + narrative, not tenure alone.

## Three loops

1. **Skill** — deepen the craft your level needs (see `roles/` + `general/`)  
2. **Scope** — take slightly larger ambiguous problems each quarter ([leveling.md](leveling.md))  
3. **Visibility** — make impact legible to your manager and adjacent leads  

Missing any loop stalls growth: strong skill with no visibility looks invisible; visibility without scope looks political.

## Promotion packet (lightweight)

Keep a living doc:

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Goals | Level you’re targeting + timeline |
| Evidence | Projects with problem → action → outcome |
| Feedback | Quotes from peers/manager; themes |
| Gaps | Honest deficits + mitigation plan |
| Asks | What you need from manager (scope, sponsors) |

Review with your manager **before** packet season, not the week of.

## Role changes

| Move | Prep |
| --- | --- |
| IC → senior IC | Broader ownership stories; mentorship |
| IC → staff | Cross-team leverage; strategy writeups |
| IC → EM | People evidence; hiring; conflict |
| Specialty switch | Bridge projects + public learning artifacts |

Treat switches like a mini job search: resume narrative + interview prep.

## Weekly habits that compound

- Ship something reviewable  
- Write down decisions and outcomes (future STAR fuel)  
- One deliberate stretch (design doc, incident lead, mentor)  
- Protect deep-work time for hard skills  

## Checklist

- [ ] Written growth goal for next 6–12 months  
- [ ] Evidence log started  
- [ ] Manager aligned on what “next level” means here  
- [ ] One stretch assignment in flight
