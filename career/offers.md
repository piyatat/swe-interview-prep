# Comparing offers

Decide with a **weighted scorecard**, not a single headline number. Pair with [negotiation.md](negotiation.md).

**Disclaimer:** Comp math below is a decision framework. Market numbers are estimates — verify with current public data.

## Build a scorecard

Pick 5–8 criteria; weight to 100%. Score each offer 1–5.

| Criterion | Example weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role / scope fit | 20 | Matches [leveling.md](leveling.md) goals |
| Team / manager | 15 | Learning and trust |
| Comp (year 1–4) | 20 | Base + bonus + equity *assumptions* |
| Growth / promo path | 10 | Ladder clarity |
| Stability / runway | 10 | Company health |
| Location / lifestyle | 10 | Commute, remote, hours |
| Mission / product | 10 | Motivation to stay |
| Risk / upside | 5 | Startup equity lottery vs cash |

Adjust weights to your life — visa and location often dominate.

## Comp sketch (keep private)

For each offer, estimate:

- Year-1 cash (base + bonus + sign-on amortized)  
- Equity: shares/options × scenario prices (bear / base / bull) — label as **scenarios**  
- Vesting cliffs and refresh expectations  
- Benefits that matter (healthcare, parental leave, etc.)  

Do not commit personal offer letters into this repo.

## Big tech vs startup tradeoffs

| | Big tech | Startup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comp shape | Higher cash predictability | More equity variance |
| Scope | Narrower early; process-heavy | Broader ownership |
| Risk | Lower employer risk | Runway / dilution / role change |
| Signal | Brand on resume | Speed of learning |

See [companies/comparison.md](../companies/comparison.md).

## Decision hygiene

- Sleep on it; don’t accept under same-day pressure if avoidable  
- Ask clarifying questions in writing  
- Revisit walk-away criteria before final yes  
- Decline politely and promptly once decided  

## Checklist

- [ ] Scorecard filled for every serious offer  
- [ ] Comp scenarios written (estimates labeled)  
- [ ] Non-comp dealbreakers listed  
- [ ] Negotiation attempt done or consciously skipped  
- [ ] Decision date met
