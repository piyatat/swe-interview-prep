# LinkedIn and outreach

Goal: a profile that matches the resume story, plus short outreach that earns replies without spam.

## Profile checklist

- **Photo / banner** — professional enough for your market; not required to be corporate stock  
- **Headline** — role + domain + proof hook (not just “Open to work”)  
  - Example: `Senior Backend Engineer | payments & reliability | ex-…`  
- **About** — 4–6 lines: who you help, strongest domains, what you’re targeting next  
- **Experience** — align with resume bullets; add links to public work  
- **Featured** — 1–3 artifacts (talk, OSS, blog, case study)  
- **Open to work** — use recruiter-only if stealth; public if actively searching  

## Weekly outreach cadence

| Channel | Volume (starting point) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm intros | 3–5 asks/week | Highest reply rate |
| Hiring managers / eng leads | 5–10 notes/week | Specific to their team |
| Recruiters | Respond fast; ask level + loop shape | Clarify timeline |
| Apply online | Cap so quality stays high | Track in pipeline ([job-search.md](job-search.md)) |

## Message shape (≤ ~5 sentences)

1. Why **them / this team** (one concrete detail)  
2. Who you are (role + strongest proof)  
3. Ask (15-min chat, referral, or “right person?”)  
4. Easy out  

### Template — hiring manager

> Hi [Name] — I saw [team/product] shipping [specific thing]. I’ve spent [N] years on [domain]; recently [1 proof with metric].  
> Open to a short chat about whether [role/team] is a fit? Happy to send a resume either way.

### Template — referral ask (warm)

> Hi [Name] — hope you’re well. I’m exploring [role type] roles and noticed [Company] is hiring for [role].  
> Would you be open to a referral or pointing me to the right eng manager? Resume attached / LinkedIn: …

## Anti-patterns

- Mass identical DMs  
- Leading with salary demands in first note  
- Attachment walls with no context  
- Pretending to know someone you don’t
