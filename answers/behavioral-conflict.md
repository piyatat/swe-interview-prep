# Behavioral: conflict with colleague — STAR outline

**Prompt:** “Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager. What did you do?”

## Probes (Amazon LP: Have Backbone; Google: collaboration)

- Did you escalate appropriately?
- Was the outcome measurable?
- What did **you** do vs the team?

## Strong STAR skeleton

| Part | Content (fill with your story) |
| --- | --- |
| **Situation** | Project, stakes, roles — 2 sentences |
| **Task** | Your specific responsibility in the disagreement |
| **Action** | 1) Heard their view 2) Data/prototype 3) Proposed experiment or doc 4) Escalation path if needed |
| **Result** | Shipped decision, metric, relationship intact |
| **Learning** | When to disagree vs defer (optional 1 line) |

## Example action bullets (template)

- Scheduled 30-min sync; listed shared goal first
- Wrote one-page comparison (latency vs complexity) with numbers
- Ran 2-day spike; presented results to team
- Committed to team decision after disagree-and-commit

## Weak vs strong

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| “They were wrong and I fixed it” | Curiosity + evidence |
| No result | Latency −20%, on-time launch |
| Pure conflict drama | Disagree-and-commit shown |

## Amazon tip

Map explicitly to **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** or **Earn Trust**.

## Timing

Target **2–3 minutes** spoken; Action is ~60%.
