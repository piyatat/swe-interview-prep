# Behavioral interviews

Behavioral rounds score **how you work with people, handle ambiguity, recover from mistakes, and drive impact** — often decisive at senior levels.

## Frameworks

### STAR (default)

| Part | Time share | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Situation** | ~15% | Context, stakes, team |
| **Task** | ~10% | Your responsibility (not the team’s generic goal) |
| **Action** | ~60% | What **you** did, decisions, tradeoffs |
| **Result** | ~15% | Measurable outcome + learning |

### CARL (alternative)

**Context → Action → Result → Learning** — useful when reflection matters more than setup.

## Story bank (prepare 8–10)

Map each story to multiple question types.

| Theme | Example prompt | Story should show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conflict | Disagreement with teammate or PM | Listening, data, compromise |
| Failure | Mistake or missed deadline | Ownership, fix, prevention |
| Leadership | Influencing without authority | Alignment, outcome |
| Incident | Production outage | Triage, comms, postmortem |
| Ambiguity | Shifting requirements | Prioritization, stakeholder mgmt |
| Mentorship | Unblocking others | Specific help, impact |
| Quality | Pushed back on risky ship | Tradeoff articulation |
| Impact | Project you’re proud of | Metrics, scope, your role |

## Common questions

### Collaboration & conflict

1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a colleague. How did you resolve it?
2. Describe a difficult stakeholder and how you worked with them.
3. Give an example of giving or receiving hard feedback.

### Ownership & failure

1. Tell me about a mistake you made in production. What happened after?
2. Describe a time you missed a deadline. What did you do?
3. When did you take on work outside your job description?

### Leadership & influence

1. Tell me about a time you improved a team process or tool.
2. How have you mentored a junior engineer?
3. Describe persuading a team to adopt your technical approach.

### Execution

1. How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?
2. Tell me about shipping under tight constraints without cutting critical quality.
3. Describe the most complex project you delivered.

## Weak vs strong signals

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| “We decided…” with no I/me | Clear personal actions |
| Vague “it went well” | Numbers, latency, revenue, time saved |
| Blaming others | Ownership + systemic fix |
| Rambling 10-min monologue | 2–3 min structured answer |

## Role adjustments

| Level | Emphasize |
| --- | --- |
| Junior | Learning speed, asking for help, careful execution |
| Mid | Ownership of features, cross-team coordination |
| Senior | Tradeoffs, mentoring, multi-quarter impact |
| Staff+ | Org-level influence, strategy, risk management |
