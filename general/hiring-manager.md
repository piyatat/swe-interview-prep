# Hiring manager round

The HM round answers: **do I want this person on my team for a year, and can I trust them with real work?** Coding and design say you *can* do the job; the manager owns the hire if it goes wrong. Typical **45–60 min**. Distinct from [recruiter-screen.md](recruiter-screen.md) (triage / band / logistics) and from a dedicated [behavioral.md](behavioral.md) panel — though the *questions* overlap.

Ask the recruiter whether this hour is **behavioral-only** or a **resume deep-dive** that turns into a mini technical interview. That changes prep.

Comp, visa, remote policy, start date: **recruiter**, not this call.

## What they are scoring

| Signal | How it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| Level / scope | End-to-end ownership vs tickets handed over |
| Ownership | Failure and incident stories use **I**, not a fog of “we” |
| Judgment | Named tradeoffs (cost you accepted), not clever uniqueness |
| Collaboration | Conflict without a villain; disagree-and-commit |
| Close-ability | Why *this* team; you evaluated them too |
| AI taste (2026) | Use + verify; not refuse-all and not paste-to-prod |

## Question shapes

### Frame (90 s)

**Prompt:** Tell me about yourself.  
**Probes:** Why this seat, not a biography.  
**Strong answer shape:** Now → through-line → why **this** role. End on the team’s problem.

### Motivation

**Prompt:** Why us / why leaving?  
**Probes:** Specific vs generic mission praise; running-from vs toward.  
**Strong answer shape:** One fact that would not apply to a competitor + one attribute of this seat. Leave without a grievance list.

### Trade-off / toughest problem

**Prompt:** Engineering trade-off; hardest technical problem you drove.  
**Probes:** What you gave up; dead ends; what “led” meant.  
**Strong answer shape:** Tension (consistency vs availability, build vs buy, debt on purpose). Messy middle. Result + what you’d change.

### Conflict / managing up

**Prompt:** Disagreement with a peer or your manager.  
**Probes:** Respect for the other side; evidence; commit after a loss.  
**Strong answer shape:** Separate person from problem. Hero/idiot scripts fail. A story where you pushed, lost, committed, and it was fine often beats a clean win.

### Failure / load

**Prompt:** Call that went wrong; everything urgent.  
**Probes:** Your part; how you told stakeholders; unsustainable load vs silent drop.  
**Strong answer shape:** Real miss, containment, prevention. “I care too much” is not a failure.

### AI in your work

**Prompt:** How do you use AI? Where don’t you trust it?  
**Probes:** A time the model was *wrong in a non-obvious way* and how you caught it.  
**Strong answer shape:** Tool with a review loop. Tie to [ai-assisted-rounds.md](ai-assisted-rounds.md) if the loop has a tool-on round.

## What to ask (last 10 min — this is scored)

1. What does success look like in six months?
2. Hardest problem the team is facing now?
3. Why is the role open?
4. How are disagreements decided?
5. What do you wish someone had told you before joining?

Skip careers-page trivia and reassurance-fishing.

## 48 h before

- [ ] 90-second intro, timed
- [ ] Every shiny resume bullet: baseline, what you changed, metric, what you’d redo
- [ ] 6–8 stories that flex (conflict, failure, trade-off, ownership) — [behavioral.md](behavioral.md)
- [ ] Engineering blog / changelog / who pays the company — enough to reason about *their* constraints
- [ ] JD verbs mapped to examples (`own` / `drive` vs `contribute`)
- [ ] Five questions for the manager

## Common failure modes

- Treating it as “not technical” and walking in cold
- Ten-minute situation, no action/result
- Badmouthing a past employer or teammate
- No questions, or asking the manager about PTO
- Staff-level title with IC-ticket stories (or the reverse)
