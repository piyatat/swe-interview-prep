# Recruiter screen (before the technical loop)

The recruiter screen is **triage**, not a formality. Typical **20–45 min**. Notes go to the hiring manager and **shape how they read your resume**. Treat it as three calls in one: **interest** (would you accept?), **fit** (does the JD match?), **level** (are you in band?). Comp details after a written offer: [offer-negotiation.md](offer-negotiation.md). Level fights: [downleveling.md](downleveling.md).

It is **not** the coding phone screen. Keep answers short. Full STAR on a logistics question sounds scripted.

## What they are scoring

| Signal | How it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative control | 60–90 s “tell me about yourself,” ending on **this role** |
| Pull vs push | Why now — toward a domain, not a grievance |
| Level | Scope: team size, what **you** owned, metric — not a feature list |
| Close-ability | Other processes, start date, location / visa, salary **band** |
| Specificity | You read the posting; you ask about **loop + HM priorities** |

Recruiters advance people they can **sell internally**. Give them 2–3 memorable facts (scope, stack, outcome).

## Question shapes (and answer length)

### Opening (interest + level)

**Prompt:** Walk me through your background / current role.  
**Probes:** Follow-ups on team size, stack, “what did *you* do?”  
**Strong answer shape:** One thread (problem → your decision → outcome). Senior answers name **shape** (“led X→Y, latency −N”), not a tour of every job.

### Motivation

**Prompt:** Why this role / why looking?  
**Probes:** Did you read the JD? Running from vs toward?  
**Strong answer shape:** Company/product (1–2 sentences) + one attribute of **this** seat. Lead with **pull**. If leaving is relevant, one unbitter sentence.

### Compensation (band fit, not negotiation)

**Prompt:** Expectations? Current TC?  
**Probes:** Can they close you in band?  
**Strong answer shape:** Ask the **posted/internal band** first. If they will not share, give a **market range** for level + metro (Levels.fyi-style aggregates), “for the right role.” Do not invent a walk-away floor. In salary-history-ban regions you need not recite current TC.

### Logistics

Notice period, location, work auth, other late-stage processes. Factual. “Actively interviewing, decide in 4–6 weeks” is a usable frame. Naming every company is optional.

## What to ask (last 8–10 min)

Ask things the recruiter **can** answer and that help you prep:

1. Loop shape: OA vs live, AI policy, debug/work-trial vs classic DSA
2. **Level this req is scoped for** — before onsite
3. What the hiring manager is optimizing for this quarter
4. Timeline and whether they run a hiring committee / bar raiser

Do not spend the call on parking and snack policy.

## Common failure modes

- Ten-minute resume monologue; “I want to grow”
- Treating the recruiter as HR-only (they still **cut**)
- Anchoring on a low current number
- Bitter current-manager stories
- No questions → looks like spray-and-pray
- Confusing this with the **technical** phone (see [prep-plan.md](prep-plan.md))

- [ ] Market band for target level + city
- [ ] Three questions about loop / level / HM
- [ ] Location, notice, visa — one-liners
